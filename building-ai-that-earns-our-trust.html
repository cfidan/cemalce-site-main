It is built, decision by decision, long before a product reaches anyone’s hands. Responsible AI starts with taking seriously who it affects. Four questions worth asking • Is it fair? Does it work equally well for different people, or does it quietly favour some? • Is it transparent? Can someone understand, at least roughly, why it reached a decision? • Is it private? Is personal data collected only when needed, and protected when it is? • Is someone accountable? When it gets something wrong, is there a person who answers for it? The goal is not machines that replace judgment, but machines that respect it. Keep humans in the loop The strongest systems pair machine speed with human judgment. For high-stakes decisions in health, finance, hiring or justice, a person should be able to review, question and overrule the result. What you can do Ask how a tool was trained. Notice when it is wrong and say so. Support products and companies that explain their choices openly.
